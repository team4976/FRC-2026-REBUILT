CURRENT CONTROLS:
-----------------

DRIVER:

    - Drive W/Left and Right Sticks
    - Brake on "A"
    - Reset Field Centric on "LB" (Left Bumper)
    - Shoot (Spin Index/Spindex) on "RT" (Right Trigger)
    - Intake on "X", is a Toggle

OPERATOR:

    - Flywheel Auto Speed on "A", Can Increase/Decrease W/Left Stick
    - Flywheel Override on "Left Stick", REQUIRES AUTO FLYWHEEL TO BE OFF
    - Shoot (Spin Index/Spindex) on "RT" (Right Trigger)
    - Auto Aim (Turret Scan) on "LT" (Left Trigger), Can Increasse/Decrease W/Right Stick
    - Manual Aim (Move Turret) on Right Stick, REQUIRES AUTO AIM TO BE OFF
    - Manual Hood on "D-Pad" (Up/Down)
    - Reverse Index/Spindex on "B"
    - Aligned Shot on "X", Aligned to the Trench Line Thing
    - Jitter Robot on "Y", Press to Jitter as Many Times as Needed
